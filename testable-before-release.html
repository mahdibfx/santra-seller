Delivery date and placed date on the order page13–14 Octneeds device

lib/models/order.dart · lib/ui/views/order_detail/order_detail_view.dart · assets/translations/{en,ar}.json

What changed. Shops could not see when an order is due or when it came in. The order page showed one line next to a calendar icon holding scheduled_slot_label — the delivery TYPE ("standard"), not a date. It now shows two labelled rows: Delivery (from delivery_date) and Placed (from created_at, converted to the phone's own timezone).

Why created_at and not received_at

received_at is when the SHOP got the order — for a card order, when the payment landed. The waiting timer counts from that. "Placed" means when the customer ordered, which is created_at. They differ by however long the customer took to pay.

How to test
  1. Open any order from the board.
  2. Under the order number, read the two rows.
  3. Check the Placed time against the real clock — it must be UAE time, not 4 hours behind.
  4. Open a PARCEL (order_kind: "delivery_service") and check both rows there too.
  5. Switch the app to Arabic and reopen.
Pass: both dates show, Placed is in local time, and the rows read right-to-left in Arabic.
Blocking on Ahmad: his interim currently stuffs the dates INTO scheduled_slot_label, which this page still renders as the window after the delivery date. If the interim is live when this ships, the line reads "Delivery: 15 Oct · 15 Oct · placed 13 Oct 14:05". He must withdraw the interim as this release goes out — he has already offered to. Afterwards it reads "Delivery: 15 Oct · 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM".
Delivery date on the board cards13 Octneeds device

lib/ui/widgets/order_card.dart

What changed. The board cards carried no date at all, so a shop planning the day had to open every order. Each card now shows the delivery date under the chips.

How to test
  1. Open the orders board with several orders on it.
  2. Each card should show a calendar line with the delivery date.
  3. Check a card for an order with no delivery date — the line should be ABSENT, not blank or wrong.
  4. Check a long shop/customer name does not push the date off the card.
  5. Switch to Arabic.
Pass: the date shows on cards that have one and is simply missing on those that do not.
